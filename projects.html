GTM Campaign

Crosschain, One Word

Across Protocol 20+ partners 1M+ organic views Ethereum Foundation

Across Protocol wanted the ecosystem to adopt "crosschain" as one word. With only a few days of lead time, we coordinated more than 20 partners, including Arbitrum, Coinbase's Base, Optimism Foundation, and the newly launched Unichain. Each team received custom branded assets for a synchronized release. At 9 a.m. PT, partners posted their "crosschain, one word, one Ethereum" animations, and the Ethereum Foundation closed the day with a compilation post. The campaign earned more than 1M organic views on X.
